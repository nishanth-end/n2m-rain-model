### Respected Sir/Madam,

We are final-year engineering students from Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE), Bengaluru, undertaking our academic Community Project titled **"Predictive Machine Learning Modeling of Hyper-Local Urban Inundation in Bengaluru Metropolitan Area"** (Course Code: 1BCP308).

The objective of our research is to build open, high-resolution, data-driven machine learning models that predict localized waterlogging and urban flash floods across BBMP ward boundaries. In benchmarking satellite-based precipitation products (CHIRPS 0.05° ~5.5 km resolution), we discovered that coarse satellite algorithms underestimate localized convective cloudbursts across Bengaluru by a factor of 3× to 13×, creating critical data gaps in micro-catchments like Mahadevapura, Bellandur, KR Puram, and Yelahanka.

KSNDMC’s dense network of Telemetric Rain Gauge (TRG) stations across the Bengaluru Urban district is widely recognized as the gold standard for hyper-local precipitation monitoring. We respectfully request your permission and assistance in obtaining historical station-level rainfall observations to train and evaluate our academic model.
