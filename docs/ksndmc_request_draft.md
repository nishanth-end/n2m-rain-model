### Respected Sir/Madam,

We are final-year engineering students from Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE), Bengaluru, working on our academic Community Project titled **"Predictive Machine Learning Modeling of Hyper-Local Urban Inundation in Bengaluru Metropolitan Area"** (Course Code: 1BCP308).

The objective of our research is to build open, high-resolution, data-driven machine learning models that can predict localized waterlogging and urban flash floods across BBMP ward boundaries. During our initial data pipeline benchmarking, we evaluated gridded satellite precipitation products (CHIRPS 0.05° ~5.5 km resolution). However, because Bengaluru’s intense flood events are driven by convective, hyper-local cloudbursts spanning localized pockets (e.g., Mahadevapura, Bellandur, KR Puram, and Yelahanka), coarse satellite data severely under-reports localized peak intensities by a factor of 3× to 10× and blurs spatial gradients across adjacent drainage micro-watersheds.

KSNDMC’s dense network of Telemetric Rain Gauge (TRG) stations across the Bengaluru Urban district is widely recognized as the gold standard for precipitation monitoring in the state. We respectfully request your permission and assistance in obtaining historical station-level rainfall data to validate and train our academic inundation model.
