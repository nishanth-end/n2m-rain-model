Title: Apartment complex flooded in pre-monsoon rain in Bengaluru
Published: 2022-05-05
Source: Deccan Herald
URL: https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fbengaluru%2Fapartment-complex-flooded-in-pre-monsoon-rain-in-bengaluru-1106860.html

Many parts of Bengaluru received light to moderate pre-monsoon showers on Thursday, disrupting normal life and inundating low-lying areas.

Parts of Rainbow Drive, an apartment complex located on Sarjapur Road, were also flooded. Bommanahalli, Mahadevapura, Yelahanka, and Bengaluru South received up to 70 mm of rainfall, while the showers were less intense in the core area, data released by the Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre (KSNDMC) on Thursday showed.
